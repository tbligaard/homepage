For Students

We offer a broad range of research-based projects at the Catalysis Theory Center and in the Bligaard Research Group.

PhD projects

If you already have, or are about to obtain, your master’s degree, and you are fluent in a branch of machine learning, in quantum mechanics or in rate theory, then get in touch and tell me about your burning desire to change the field of science and the world for the better.

M.Sc. projects

Students wishing to carry out their M.Sc. project in the group will typically, in addition to the B.Sc. background below, have followed at least one of the courses Electronic Structure Methods, Machine Learning and Materials Design or Basic Concept in Heterogeneous Catalysis – and applications to energy conversion and storage.

B.Sc. projects

The typical B.Sc. project student has taken an introduction to Quantum Mechanics or Quantum Chemistry, or has some training in Bayesian Statistics. Physics students typically follow the Solid State Physics course during their B.Sc. project.

Special student projects

Students who want to brush up on their skills in group theory, quantum mechanics, solid state physics, electronic structure theory, heterogeneous catalysis, rate theory, Bayesian statistics, search methods accelerated by surrogate machine-learning models, global and local optimization, programming, atomistic simulation methods or Gaussian processes are welcome to drop by for a chat. We typically advise you to follow the standard DTU courses on these topics, but we understand the challenges of modern course scheduling and are happy to help, on an individual basis, to fill any educational gaps this causes.
