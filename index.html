Bligaard Research Group

Our research goal is to understand and design functional materials through atomic-scale computer simulations, and to establish the tools to do so. The methods we use are primarily machine learning, quantum mechanics, density functional theory, numerical analysis, statistical mechanics, kinetic modelling and rate theory.

The Bligaard research group is located on the top floor of the Climate Challenge Laboratory, Building 313, on DTU's Lyngby campus. It is one of four research groups in the Catalysis Theory Center, part of the SurfCat section at the Department of Physics. The group also participates in the VISION center and the CAPeX Pioneer Center.

Fundamental Concepts in Heterogeneous Catalysis

J. K. Nørskov, F. Studt, F. Abild-Pedersen and T. Bligaard · Wiley, 2014

An introduction to the modern theory of heterogeneous catalysis, from potential energy diagrams and kinetics to scaling relations, activity maps and the d-band model.
