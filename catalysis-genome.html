Catalysis Genome

The quest for the materials genome — the properties of a material that define its functional properties — has started. This signifies a transition to a new era of materials research, in which large amounts of materials data become available. The expectation is that this will significantly speed up the discovery of new materials.

Read more: J. K. Nørskov and T. Bligaard, The Catalyst Genome, Angewandte Chemie International Edition 52, 776–777 (2013).
